PESCHE-FU · RESEARCH

The research

Developing smart photoelectrocatalytic systems for the sustainable production of chemicals and fuels.

SCIENTIFIC APPROACH

A paired photoelectrocatalytic approach

PESCHE-FU proposes a new tandem photoelectrocatalytic strategy in which the photooxidation of biomass-derived molecules is coupled with a tunable electroreduction process.

The approach aims to combine solar light and renewable electricity to drive useful chemical transformations under environmentally friendly conditions, with water as a preferred solvent.

RESEARCH AREAS

Four interconnected research areas

01

Smart Photoanodes

Objective

Development of smart nanostructured photoanodes for the selective photooxidation of biomass-derived molecules.

Materials

Graphitic carbon nitride (g-CN), defects, single atoms, geminal atoms, and semiconductor heterojunctions.

Target Products

Acetic acid and oxalic acid obtained through sustainable solar-driven conversion pathways.

02

Smart Electrocatalysts

Objective

Development of electrocatalysts with tunable selectivity towards hydrogen evolution and electrochemical hydrogenation reactions.

Materials

CeO₂-based hybrid catalysts, conductive carbon nanostructures, metal nanoparticles and single-atom catalysts.

Target Products

Hydrogen and hydrogenated molecules derived from olefins, phenols and biomass-related substrates.

03

Structure–Activity Relationships

Objective

Understand how catalyst structure governs activity, selectivity and long-term stability.

Methods

Advanced ex situ, in situ and operando characterization combined with mechanistic investigations.

Outcome

Design rules for the development of next-generation smart catalytic materials and PEC systems.

04

Integrated PEC Device

Objective

Integrate photooxidation and electrocatalyticreduction into a single photoelectrochemical platform.

Strategy

Couple smart photoanodes and smart electrocatalysts within a tandem PEC architecture powered by solar light and renewable electricity.

Goal

Demonstrate the simultaneous production of value-added chemicals and clean fuels in a prototype photoelectrochemical device.
