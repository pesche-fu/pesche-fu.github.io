5/10/2026

Sustainable Materials and Materials for Sustainability — A Successful Kick-off Meeting

On 2 October 2026, the kick-off meeting “Sustainable Materials and Materials for Sustainability” was held at the Department of Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences. The event brought together the researchers involved in the two FIS-funded projects hosted by the Department, PESCHE-FU and PHOTOHYDRO.

During the meeting, the research activities of the two projects were presented, together with keynote presentations from international experts on sustainability and new hybrid functional materials. We would like to thank all the speakers and participants for their valuable contributions and for making the meeting a stimulating and fruitful occasion for scientific exchange. Our sincere thanks go to Prof. Paolo Fornasiero and Prof. Federico Rosei, Principal Investigators of the two projects, for their scientific leadership and continued support. We are also grateful to Rector Prof. Donata Vianelli and to Prof. Paolo Tecilla, Director of the Department of Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences, for their support and for contributing to the success of the event.
